Parent FAQ

A few answers before you write. If your question is not here, email me and I will reply.

What ages do you work with?

I coach youth players in travel and rec baseball. Each session fits the player's age and skill level. Put the player's age on the lesson request, and I will confirm by email.

What should my player bring?

A baseball glove, and a bat and helmet if your player has them. Wear athletic shoes or cleats, and bring water. Dress for an outdoor practice unless I tell you otherwise when I confirm the lesson.

How do I request a lesson, and how is it confirmed?

Fill out the lesson request form on this page. I reply by email to confirm a time.

How long are lessons, and what do they cost?
  • 30 minutes: $30
  • 60 minutes: $50
  • 4-lesson pack (60 min each): $180
Do you offer small-group lessons?

Yes. I teach one-on-one and small-group lessons. The prices above are the ones listed on this page. If you want a small group, tell me how many players and what you want to work on, and I will confirm the details by email.

Where are lessons held?

The location is arranged by email when the lesson is booked. I coach in Crawfordsville, Indiana.

How do I reschedule or cancel?

Email or message me at least 24 hours ahead.

How do I reach you?

Use the contact form on this page. I reply by email.
