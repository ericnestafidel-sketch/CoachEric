Practice Plans

Three complete 90-minute indoor practice plans for 9–10 year olds, built from real practices I run. Each one has a warm-up, a main block, and a closing game. Pitching stays low-workload all winter: mechanics and throwing strikes only, no max effort.

90 minutesIndoor · Ages 9–10

Plan 1 — Stations + Pitcher/Catcher Lab

Focus: pitching and catching form, plus tee, grounder, and fly-ball work.

  1. 0:00–0:05 — Dynamic warm-up, keep stretching short.
  2. 0:05–0:15 — Throwing progression for everyone.
  3. 0:15–1:15 — Pitcher/catcher lab plus stations, in 4 waves of 15 minutes. Two pitchers and two catchers work live at a time: one pitcher pitches live while the other throws balls for the other catcher to block, swapping after about 7 minutes. The rest of the team rotates tee, grounder, and fly-ball stations in small groups.
  4. 1:15–1:30 — Target Bunt Challenge to close: two teams, coach soft-tosses wiffle balls. A bunt into the target boxes down the 1st or 3rd base line scores 2 points, a fair bunt outside the boxes scores 1, and a foul, pop-up, or miss scores 0. Each hitter gets 5 bunts.

Coaching point: each pitcher gets about 14 minutes of real work; each catcher gets about 30 minutes across two waves. Keep the other stations mostly self-running so you can coach the lab.

90 minutesIndoor · Ages 9–10

Plan 2 — Footwork + Diving

Focus: first steps, moving through the ball, and getting comfortable leaving the feet.

  1. 0:00–0:05 — Dynamic warm-up.
  2. 0:05–0:15 — Throwing progression for everyone.
  3. 0:15–0:25 — Hash drill for everyone: players move side to side on the hash marks in short bursts, glove down, feet first.
  4. 0:25–1:05 — Half-court drills, twice through in 10-minute blocks: fly balls with a crow hop on one side, ground balls on the other, swapping every 10 minutes. The pitcher/catcher lab runs alongside in 4 waves of 10 minutes and ends early at 1:05.
  5. 1:05–1:20 — Diving all together: diving for balls on pads, then dive-backs to 1st base on a leadoff.
  6. 1:20–1:30 — Bomb Squad, an optional coach-led closing game, only if time allows.

Coaching point: a player who is not in the lab gets each half-court drill twice; a player who was in the lab still gets each drill at least once.

90 minutesIndoor · Ages 9–10

Plan 3 — Baserunning, Hitting & Signs

Focus: reading the ball, running the bases, and learning team signs.

  1. 0:00–0:05 — Dynamic warm-up.
  2. 0:05–0:15 — Throwing progression for everyone.
  3. 0:15–0:35 — Teach the signs: indicator, sac bunt, swing away, hit and run, squeeze, and take. Walk through what each one asks the batter and the runners to do.
  4. 0:35–1:15 — The 5-pitch sequence, live: foam balls with metal bats, runners on 1st and 2nd. Each batter gets 5 pitches — sac bunt, swing away, swing away, hit and run, squeeze bunt — and must confirm the squeeze sign before that pitch.
  5. 1:15–1:30 — Defense and wrap: a few situational reps, then break.

Coaching point: the read comes off the batted ball, not a guess before contact. On a fly ball, find it before leaving the base area; tag up when it is caught.

Pitch Smart, ages 9–10: max 75 pitches a day. Rest days by pitch count: 1–20 needs none, 21–35 needs 1, 36–50 needs 2, 51–65 needs 3, 66 or more needs 4. A player should not pitch and catch in the same game. Always follow your league's rules where they differ.

Coming next: signs under pressure, a first introduction to pickoff moves (after the 4th practice), and simulated innings that put it all together.
